## The model, restated

- **Who acts.** One child at a time (several siblings enrolled, or an unidentified guest) at the desk; the parent through a web UI and CLI; the Brain (Claude, magpie-routed providers, Ollama) reached only through `leafi.privacy.egress`; the study harness, one Python process per session holding the speech lease, beside the supervisor and the web UI, all writing one SQLite file.
- **States.** Session owner (guest → confirmed learner, or guest → `anonymous` counts row at finalize); cycle `open/closed`; activity `running → final status`; the epoch tuple; the monotonic terminal latch and the skip queue; the ledger; consent `granted → used`; egress attempt `sending → sent/failed/unknown`; miscue and knowledge-item statuses; per-learner `cloud.text`.
- **Who decides.** The Brain proposes the next activity at a boundary; the harness builds the menu, constructs, validates, times and limits; the learner accepts or refuses according to Leafi's autonomy level; `egress` decides, at send time, from current settings and provenance, what leaves; the parent approves cards and corrections and can forget a learner.
- **Outsiders.** No public surface. Ids are local integers. The web UI listens on 127.0.0.1 with no authentication, so "an outsider" here means two things: a cloud provider receiving payloads, and a child at the household PC who can open the parent's pages.
- **Stack.** Python 3.12 (asyncio, httpx, `anthropic` 0.120.2, edge-tts), SQLite through `leafi/core/storage.py`, which applies migrations inside `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` with `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON`. No binding file matches (see Materials).

## Outside the catalog

- **Deletion across stores.** `forget --learner` is specified for SQLite and `data/learners/<id>/` only. Learner-derived content also lives in Anki notes in the learner's deck, in `cache/tts` clips of lines that quote or answer the learner (90-day retention), in `data/evals/decisions/` replay observations, in magpie's local logs and in `LEAFI_DEBUG_TEXT` logs. No question asks whether a deletion right covers every store that holds a copy (decision 5).
- **Anki sync is an egress path outside the table.** If the household syncs Anki to AnkiWeb, card text (context sentences, explanations, possibly derived from learner text) leaves through a loopback client (AnkiConnect) that neither `egress` nor the socket guard can see. This predates these ADRs.
- **Binding candidate `sqlite.md`.** The migration trap in G4.2 is a SQLite-specific shape of G4.2/G4.3 that a binding file would carry.
