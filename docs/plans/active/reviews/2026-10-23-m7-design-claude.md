# M7 design rev 1 — fresh Claude session (round 1)

Reviewer: a fresh Claude agent on `reviews/prompts/m7-design.md`, independent of the other two reviewers; it read `egress.py` (`prepare`, `_gate`, `admit`, `MediatingTransport._send`), `destinations.py`, `latch.py`, `ledger.py`, `busy.py`, the brain backends, the schema-6 DDL and the installed `anthropic` SDK. Date: 2026-10-23.

## Findings

1. **High · D4/D9 — the filter and egress's gate disagree for Ollama.** `destinations.registry` marks the `ollama` destination `local`; `prepare` gates and pseudonymises only when `dest.locality == "cloud"`; nothing reads `LOCAL_MODELS`. An `ollama:<cloud-hosted model>` tier, or `OLLAMA_URL` on another machine, receives a `cloud.text`-off learner's words unpseudonymised. Fix: locality per (destination, model) in `prepare`, cloud unless on `LOCAL_MODELS`; or refuse such a tier; test. *(Verified by the author 2026-10-23: `LOCAL_MODELS` is parsed (`config.local_model_list`) and in `versions.POLICY_KEYS` but enforced nowhere. Latent today: the v1 Ollama adapter sends page text only and `GATE_FLOOR` blocks `BRAIN_BACKEND=ollama`.)*
2. **High · D6 and output provenance.** The rejection line is `system` but a Pydantic error echoes `input_value='…'` (a local tier's output may echo heard words, then go to a cloud tier). Nothing assigns `response_provenance_class`; `say_before`, answers, explanations and cards built from a payload with learner text must be `derived_from_learner`, else TTS routing sends them to edge-tts and they return as `system` in later observations. Fix: reason codes plus ids; every output takes the strongest class and the sources of the payload sent.
3. **High · D14 — replay loses provenance;** plain strings re-prepared as `system`; with provenance kept but no filter, every such line is refused. Fix: leaves `{value, provenance, sources}`, replay through `brain_call`'s filter under current settings.
4. **Medium · D4 — whole-section drop narrows ADR-0018 §2** (expected words + category); `last_activity` and `memory` vanish at nearly every boundary for a `cloud.text`-off learner, biasing §9. Fix: per-leaf filter.
5. **Medium · D8 — `Latch.spawn` returns no handle;** a filler exception ends the session `internal_error`; a reply at 0.85 s races the filler's `say` (cut mid-phrase or two speakers); a late filler can speak after the epoch bump. Fix: a local task group / cancel scope, errors logged, epoch checked, filler speech awaited.
6. **Medium · D5 — the version check and the reservation are one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` in a busy-retry loop;** charging between them double-charges on a busy retry and charges for nothing on a stale-versions return (today `self.budget` runs before the version check). Fix: charge once after the reservation commits. Units of `extract_calls` 2 unspecified (deep = 3 never affordable; one retry spends standard's second unit).
7. **Medium · cancellation and timeouts.** The `brain_calls` row under a cancelled scope is lost when the store is busy (shield it). A `decide` timeout on standard leaves ≈ 2 s for deep, which cannot finish: ≈ 4 units wasted per bad boundary, 40 units gone in ≈ 10. Fix: skip a tier whose expected latency exceeds the remaining deadline; escalate up only on invalid shapes, prefer a faster tier after a timeout. `reset()` is synchronous and cannot `aclose` the old client; `over_budget` vs `refused` needs the 403 body read.
8. **Medium · D3 — thinking tokens count toward `max_tokens`;** judge 512 / word 256 on Opus 5.5 will stop at `max_tokens`. Fix: headroom, cap the reply by schema; measure.
9. **Medium · D3 schemas.** A Pydantic discriminated union emits `oneOf` + `discriminator` (unsupported); optional fields emit `anyOf[…, null]` + `default`; an 11-kind `anyOf` with optional content may exceed the vendor's complexity limits (reviewer's recollection, to be checked); a 301-char `say_before` fails the whole `Decision` though D10 discards only content. Fix: strict selection, loose content in `decide`, content validated per kind at construction; a test that the SDK body equals the prepared body with the real schema; the schema byte-identical across sessions (24 h compile cache).
10. **Medium · D11 — the `cycle` section carries keys, not ids;** a real but unrelated `item_id` credits the wrong item; answerability drops open-ended and distancing questions. Fix: render ids; each cited item's key must occur in its question; define open-ended scoring (`unavailable`, no event); reject stop-word-only answer sets.
11. **Medium · D13 — status not derivable:** `ignored`, Anki interval and lapses not in `retrieval_events`; `learning → lapsed` undefined in `retrievals` mode. Fix: an item-status event shape (new data shape: research first).
12. **Low · D4 caching** — `session` first caps the cached prefix; no breakpoint named.
13. **Low · the epoch string carries `session_id` and `learner_id`** — echo an opaque turn token.
14. **Low · ADR items with no step:** the `degraded` preface; notes dropping out after 10 sessions and `forget --session` deleting notes; the magpie probe; `test_card_checks` / `test_anki_single_path` with Brain-owned content; console buttons. M7a–M7e: say the v1 `run(BrainRequest)` and the port coexist until M7e; say which provenance type TTS uses.
15. **Low · a `cloud.text` change between the filter and `prepare`** → `Refused` (safe); record it as `refused`.

## Sound

D1 (the port; `leafi.core.text` exists and privacy may import core); D3 `messages.create` over `parse` (SDK 1.17's `OutputConfigParam` has `effort` and `format`); D5's per-token hook once placed after the reservation; D7 "payload as prepared, response before reverse"; D8's camera simplification (§5.2 allows Face text); D9's order; D10's epoch handling; D12; D15; `answer`/`judge` as eligibility; guests skipped; one charge per transmission.
