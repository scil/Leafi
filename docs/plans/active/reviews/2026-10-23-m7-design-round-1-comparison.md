## Owner decisions (rev 2 waits on these)

- **O1 Tier for `decide`.** Sonnet 5.5 (`standard`, as the ADR's table) at ≈ 10 boundaries a session, or Haiku 5.5 until measured.
- **O2 The replay set.** All three reviewers: off by default, opt-in under the existing evaluation-data retention; replay sends to a cloud tier only through the same filter (or local backends only).
- **O3 Schema warm-up** at session start (no learner data), or accept a default-chain first boundary.
- **O4 Tier keys.** Amend ADR-0018 from one `BRAIN_TIERS` key to three keys (the author's proposal), or keep one key.
- **O5 YLE.** Build the YLE parser in M7 (a PDF library: a new dependency, research first) or defer it explicitly with CYP-LEX alone until then.
- **O6 Ranking vocabulary.** Does an item in `learning` count as known when finding one-unknown sentences (the card checker says no)?

**Answered 2026-10-23:** O1 Haiku 5.5 first (against the recommendation); O2 on by default (against the reviewers' recommendation); O5 deferred; O6 `learning` counts as unknown. O3 and O4 taken as the author's calls (no warm-up, byte-identical schema; three tier keys with the ADR-0018 amendment); the owner may override. Recorded in the plan's Decision log.
