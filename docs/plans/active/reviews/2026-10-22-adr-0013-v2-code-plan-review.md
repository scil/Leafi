# code-plan-review: ADR-0013 v2 (reading algorithm), round 1

Reviewer: an independent Claude session (Opus 5.5) running `code-plan-review` from the catalog alone (Python 3.12 + SQLite; there is no binding for either), 2026-10-22, one of two independent reviewers. Scope is the same as the codex prompt [prompts/adr-0013-v2-round-1.md](prompts/adr-0013-v2-round-1.md). The settled points were not argued: practice comes after reading, cycle-owned candidates, Leafi's own DP, and `confirmed` needs acoustic evidence.

## The model, restated

- **Who acts and what goes in.** One learner reads a physical page aloud, or types. ASR utterances (words with confidences) go into a pure `align(state, utterance, info)` over the tokens of the current page revision.
- **State.** Per-token statuses U/P/C/M/S/R, a frontier `pcur`, a commit cursor `ccur`, `completed` sentences, `turn_id` and `off_task_run`. The state is persisted per page read: one per `(cycle, page, revision)`.
- **Alignment.** A fast windowed DP runs first. A page-wide DP with one flat entry jump runs when the fast path is not accepted, or when its cost is over 0.5 per heard word. The chosen path must carry an anchor (rules 4(a)–(c)) or the utterance is `off_task`.
- **Candidates.** Substitutions and short deletions on content words become candidates owned by the cycle. A later match withdraws them. `practise_errors` practises them after reading, and `close_cycle` settles what is left.
- **What follows from alignment.** Sentence completion, `/back`, page turns, corrections, Difficulties and the summary all follow from the token statuses and the miscue rows.
- **Outsiders.** None. It is a single-household, single-process loop with no public path. B3/C3/C4 are judged against that.

## Decisions for the owner

1. **Slow readers (K1.1 row 3, H7.2 row 1).** Should completion (ii) wait while content words remain (RA1), and can a lone substituted content word at `pcur` ever become a candidate? *Recommendation:* (ii) fires only when no content word of the sentence is still `unattempted`. Lead silence is handled by ADR-0017 §6.1's ladder. A lone substitution at `pcur` with confidence ≥ `ANCHOR_CONF` is recorded as an observation and leaves the token `unattempted`, so the next utterance can reach it. Whether it becomes a candidate is decided with the false-alarm eval.
2. **Told words (I2.1 row 1).** Should a `supplied` candidate survive the learner then reading the word? *Recommendation:* yes. Supplied candidates are exempt from rule 7's withdrawal, following the running-record convention that a told word is an error.
3. **`interrupted` in Difficulties (K2.1 row 2).** v2 candidates are never practised before close, so `unsure(interrupted)` carries the same evidence as `not_practised`. *Recommendation:* `v(s) = 0` for `interrupted`, like `not_practised`.
4. **`item_said` (I2.1 row 2).** Does the practice retry ("some heard word is the target") or a whole-answer test own `item_said`? *Recommendation:* ADR-0017 §3.4 states its own whole-answer rule, built from the pair classes, and stops borrowing the retry test.
5. **Inflection test (H7.1 row 2).** Should it be a lemmatiser or inflection table, or the suffix-stripping rule? *Recommendation:* use an inflection library (e.g. lemminflect) or at least restrict `d` to stems ending in `e`. Record the search either way.
6. **Page turns over a sentence that is not finished (F1.1 row 1).** *Recommendation:* (iii) on a page turn completes nothing that has no aligned token. A sentence with aligned tokens is left open (not completed) when the activity ends `page_turned`, so a resumed page read continues inside it.

## Most severe

No finding in this review is about disclosure or privacy. Three findings are High.

| rank | row | severity | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | K1.1 (slow reader) | **High** | For a child reading one or two words per utterance, a 1.2 s pause with `pcur` at `last − 1` completes the sentence and marks two read words `unread`. A one-word misreading is `off_task`, and the token later becomes `unread`. A word skipped at an utterance start is never a deletion. So detection fails systematically for the expected reader, and the records say "unread / off-task". |
| 2 | F1.1 (page turn by (iii)) | **High** | Turning away from a page completes its current sentence even when nothing in it was read, as in walkthrough (k) on B. Its tokens become `unread` and stay `completed`. On return, the fast window starts after it, so every reread needs the page pass (R = 3). Tokens matched there become `provisional`, and no rule ever commits them. This leaves a reachable state with no exit. |
| 3 | G2.2 (token index after correction) | **High** | `close_cycle` and Practice address `tokens[m.expected_token_index]`, an index under revision r, in a page-read state the Correction interaction has re-tokenised onto r+1. A correction that splits or merges a word before the candidate (common in OCR fixes) settles the wrong token and leaves the real candidate `M`. The persisted reading state is corrupted. |
| 4 | I2.1 (supplied word) | Medium | A word Leafi supplies is a candidate with `heard = null`. The learner then reads it, and rule 7 withdraws it (`self_corrected` or `reread`). Told words are therefore never practised, which defeats the convention the ADR adopts. |
| 5 | I2.1 (`item_said`) | Medium | ADR-0017 §3.4 scores `item_said` with "ADR-0013's retry test". v2's test passes when *some* heard word is the target, so "I don't know what through means" is a success. ADR-0017's own Confirmation (`test_answer_scores.py`) asserts the opposite. |

The other Medium rows are: heard-number collapse (false alarms on spelled-out numbers); inflection over-match (car/card hidden); the page pass broken by dropped articles; rule 5's line skip having no status filter; walkthrough (g)'s cost and acceptance claim being wrong; `interrupted` weighting; the `practices.attempt` CHECK across page turns; and Practice vs the Correction interaction on corrected revisions.
