# ADRs are short

New ADRs follow the format of Matt Pocock's `domain-modeling` skill (`ADR-FORMAT.md`): a title and as few paragraphs as the decision needs, with `status` frontmatter kept so a decision can be superseded. This replaces ADR-0005's "MADR shape" for new ADRs; ADR-0001…0014 keep their MADR sections and are not rewritten. The owner chose the short format over docs-maintainer's MADR shape because the value is recording that a decision was made and why, not filling sections.
